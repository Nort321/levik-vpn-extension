# English listing

**Name:** Levik VPN

**Short description:** Encrypted Chrome browsing with smart routing and a 3-day free trial.

**Description:**

A more open internet, right from your browser. Levik VPN brings a clean, simple connection control to Chrome.

- Encrypted browser-to-server connection through an HTTPS proxy.
- Full browser routing, or your own list of domains and subdomains.
- Include selected sites or bypass them with smart routing.
- A 72-hour free trial, without a payment card, subject to eligibility.
- Sign-in through the leviknet.org personal cabinet and a clear access countdown.
- English and Russian, with instant language switching.
- Optional WebRTC protection and startup connection.

Levik VPN protects supported Chrome browsing, not other apps or all system traffic. Direct/bypassed sites and some Chrome background traffic use your normal connection. Available locations depend on the deployed service. No ad blocker is included. A Levik account or eligible trial and Internet access are required. Pro access is offered separately through Levik; the extension does not charge or auto-renew your subscription.

**Single purpose:** User-controlled encrypted browser proxy routing.

**Reviewer instructions:** Use a time-limited personal-cabinet test account supplied privately in the dashboard, connect, open an HTTPS website, test a smart-routing domain, then disconnect. Check Settings for Russian/English and privacy disclosures. Do not submit before the gateway and account workflow are live.
