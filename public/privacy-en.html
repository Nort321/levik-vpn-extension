Levik VPN Privacy Policy

Extension version 1.0.8 · 15 October 2026

This policy describes the Levik VPN Chrome extension and its standalone gateway. Contact the Levik team through the account/support interface at leviknet.org.

What stays on your device

The extension stores your selected language, location, routing rules, connection preferences, account session, trial status and pending cabinet-pairing information in Chrome's local extension storage. These settings are not synced. Sign-in takes place in your existing leviknet.org personal cabinet; its password and cookies are never transferred to the extension. Session duration is calculated locally; complete byte totals and browsing history are not recorded.

What the service processes

To sign in or activate a trial, the extension creates a short-lived pairing request and opens the Levik personal cabinet. After your explicit approval, the cabinet sends your account identifier, account label and eligible subscription expiry directly to the gateway over authenticated TLS. The gateway stores account labels, entitlement dates, hashed session tokens and hashed account trial-eligibility identifiers. Pairing requests expire after five minutes and are single-use. Source IP addresses are processed in memory for rate limits. The gateway periodically checks whether the account remains active. Proxy destinations and connection data must be processed for routing and are held in memory during a connection; the supplied code does not persist browsing destinations or page content in access logs.

When the extension is linked to a Levik account, the gateway receives the account’s site rules from the personal cabinet (domains that go through the VPN or directly) and keeps them in memory only. The “This site” buttons send the domain of the open tab to your account only when you press them; the rule is stored in the account until you remove it and is applied by your Levik apps and router too. Rules take priority over the connection mode.

When connecting, the extension requests https://example.com through the proxy to check connectivity. That destination receives a normal request from the gateway; no account token is sent to the destination. The Levik API and configured proxy hostname bypass routing so account management remains reachable. In Smart routing, Russian domain zones and your chosen additional exceptions use direct connections; Chrome's implicit local-address exceptions are also direct.

The extension requests the public server inventory from sub.leviknet.org on opening and every minute, including before sign-in. This direct HTTPS request contains no account credentials or browsing history; the server necessarily receives your network IP address. The inventory service does not persist request logs and returns only current members of the Levik Runtime Xray squad.

Encryption and scope

Browser-to-gateway traffic is encrypted with TLS. HTTPS website content stays encrypted between Chrome and the destination; ordinary HTTP is not encrypted between the gateway and destination. The gateway can process destination hosts and network metadata. Other apps, direct traffic and some Chrome background traffic are outside the extension's scope. The optional WebRTC setting disables non-proxied UDP while connected and may affect calls. There is no ad blocker, analytics SDK, sale of data, advertising personalization or third-party tracking code in the extension.

Retention and choices

Local preferences remain until you remove the extension or clear its data. Signing out clears the local session and requests server revocation; an offline request may not reach the server, where the session still expires automatically. Paid sessions expire on the active Regular or Multi subscription date; trial sessions expire after 72 hours. In-memory rate-limit records expire after one minute. Account linkage and hashed trial-eligibility records remain in the gateway database until the operator removes them; they prevent reuse and support account administration. Expired pairing records are removed on a new pairing request; secret polling tokens are only stored as hashes on the server. You can request deletion through Levik support. The deployed operator must disclose any additional infrastructure logs, retention periods, payment providers or account integrations before launch.

Your control

Activation and login require consent to this policy. You can disconnect at any time, change routing rules, disable automatic connection, sign out or uninstall the extension. Disconnection releases the proxy and WebRTC settings controlled by this extension. Revocation and expiry are enforced by the gateway. The trial lasts 72 hours and does not request a payment card or automatically charge you.
